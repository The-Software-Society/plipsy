# Locked illustration style for Mia v1

This prompt prefix is prepended to every illustration generation request.
Editing it here updates the next regeneration run; existing assets do not
change unless `--force` is passed.

## Style prefix

Soft watercolor children's book illustration. Muted earthy palette of sage
green, terracotta, warm cream, dusty blue, and soft mustard. Gentle hand
drawn linework with visible brush texture. Warm soft natural lighting, no
hard shadows. Generous negative space. Calm Sarah and Duck or Bluey
aesthetic, never CoComelon. Square 1 to 1 composition. One subject
centered. No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermarks, no signatures.

## Locked character descriptions

These descriptions are inlined into prompts so the recurring characters
look consistent across scenes.

### Mia
A 3 year old girl with light brown curly hair tied in TWO SEPARATE LOW
PIGTAILS, one on each side of her head, short and bunched. She never has
loose flowing hair, a single ponytail, or a bun. Her eyes are large, round
and solid warm brown with a single bright catchlight, drawn simply with no
visible eyelashes. Freckles across the nose, round pink cheek blush, soft
round cheeks. She wears a dusty blue overall dress over a soft cream long
sleeved shirt and warm terracotta cardigan. Her stuffed toy is a small soft
sage green rabbit with cream ears.

### The cat
A small orange tabby kitten with white socks and a soft round face,
gentle expression, slightly fluffy.

### The dog
A medium fluffy brown dog with soft floppy ears, kind eyes, a happy
gently wagging tail, a small cream patch on the chest.

### The dinosaur
A tiny friendly green dinosaur the size of a small house cat, large round
eyes, soft tummy, two tiny arms, a single dusty blue stripe down the back.
Looks more like a stuffed animal than a real dinosaur.
