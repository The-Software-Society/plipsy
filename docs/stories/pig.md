## At a glance
- **Hero word:** PIG
- **Target words:** BIG · DIG · PIG
- **Review words:** SIT
- **Phonics focus:** short I · `-ig` family
- **Moral:** use what makes you special to help — the pig's size/strength saves the day.
- **Characters:** Mia, **Rosie** (pig, debut)

## Structure
One branch (big vs dig), reconverging into PIG (hero) and a SIT review.

```
s1 → s2 ─choice─┬ BIG → s3a ─┐
                └ DIG → s3b ─┴→ s4 (spell PIG, hero) → s5 (spell SIT, review) → s6 (end)
```

## Scene-by-scene

### s1 — ball in the mud
- *"Oh no! The ball is stuck IN the mud."* — tone: soft — highlights: **IN**
- *"It is too deep! No one can reach."* — tone: playful
- → continue to s2

### s2 — who can help
- **Choice — "Who is big and strong? Who can help dig?"**
  - **BIG** → s3a
  - **DIG** → s3b

### s3a — big
- *"The pig is big and strong! I can help!"* — tone: cheer
- **Spell BIG** — prompt *"Spell BIG!"* → s4

### s3b — dig
- *"The pig digs and digs. Mud splats. Splat, splat!"* — tone: giggle
- **Spell DIG** — prompt *"Spell DIG!"* → s4

### s4 — saved (hero)
- *"Up comes the ball! Rosie the PIG saved the day."* — tone: cheer — highlights: **PIG**
- **Spell PIG** ⭐ hero — prompt *"Spell PIG!"* → s5

### s5 — rest (review)
- *"The big pig can SIT and rest now."* — highlights: **SIT**
- **Spell SIT** (review) — prompt *"Spell SIT!"* → s6

### s6 — end
- *"The pig used what made it special."* — tone: warm
- *"Being you can help your friends!"* — tone: warm
- → end
